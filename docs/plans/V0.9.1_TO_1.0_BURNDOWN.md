# Pivotglass v0.9.1 to v1.0 burndown

This plan keeps the remaining work small enough to verify and release. A target
version is a scheduling boundary, not a claim that the work is complete.

## v0.9.1 — Analyst Flow

- Make the focused, single-column investigation view the default.
- Put the current question, recent change, separate progress measures, open
  gaps, and one deterministic next action in the first viewport.
- Keep all expert analysis, configuration, visualization, and presentation
  controls available through four primary destinations.
- Verify phone, laptop, Day, Night, keyboard, and no-overflow behavior.

## v0.9.2 — Flint 0.4 qualification and evidence clusters

**Candidate status (2026-08-31):** implementation complete pending the frozen
release gates recorded in [the v0.9.2 quality record](../QA_V0.9.2.md). Flint
0.4 is pinned and analytically compared; the 0.3 rollback path is rehearsed;
evidence clusters and the shared command are implemented; and document intake
is deliberately browser-preview-only. Persistent export/purge and broader
format support remain scheduled rather than being implied by this release.

Qualify Flint 0.4.0 first so the remaining pre-1.0 releases provide meaningful
soak time and leave a safe rollback window:

- Upgrade `flint-chart` from 0.3.0 to exactly 0.4.0 in an isolated change that
  preserves the 0.3.0 lockfile, built web export, and rollback instructions.
- Compile every allow-listed Pivotglass visualization intent against both
  versions with the same seeded data. Compare the exact plotted-data export,
  field semantics, transforms, domains, aggregation, ordering, labels, color,
  and empty/error behavior—not only screenshots.
- Re-run visual, keyboard, screen-reader, high-contrast, Day/Night,
  reduced-motion, 320 px, laptop, and full-screen checks for every supported
  visualization family and renderer.
- Treat Plotly, editable Excel charts, and other Flint 0.4 capabilities as
  separately qualified backends or exports; their presence in the dependency
  does not make them production defaults.
- Produce dependency, license, build, accessibility, and rollback receipts.
  Rehearse restoring 0.3.0 and its static export before accepting 0.4.0.
- Revert to 0.3.0 if 0.4.0 changes analytical meaning, breaks accessibility or
  supported browsers, creates an unresolved supply-chain issue, or threatens
  the v1.0 release. Do not maintain two drifting runtime selection paths.

After Flint qualification:

- Establish the versioned document-ingestion envelope, content-addressed
  original-document store, parser receipts, size limits, and preview-only
  command/UI path for text, Markdown, HTML, PDF, CSV, JSON, JSONL, and email.
- Summarize connected components of admitted graph edges as evidence or
  activity clusters with stable identifiers.
- Show contributing entities, edge truth classes, provenance diversity,
  first/last observed time, mapped behaviors, and Dossier gaps.
- Never turn a cluster into an actor attribution without an explicit,
  sourced analyst judgment and formal confidence.

## v0.9.3 — Longitudinal tracking

**Candidate status (2026-08-31):** deterministic exact-span extraction and
local graph-cluster history are implemented. Candidates preserve occurrence,
parser receipt, character and byte span, line/column, context, normalized
value, rule version, and candidate state; they do not enter STIX or the graph.
Snapshots and diffs are bounded and shared by the TUI and web command paths.
Reviewed SCOT publication and live Synapse-backed comparison remain preview
integration gates rather than claims of local completion.

- Add deterministic entity extraction with exact document, page, span, byte or
  character offsets, normalized value, parser/rule version, and surrounding
  context. Extracted entities remain candidates until admission policy accepts
  them.
- Track cluster changes across investigation sessions without rewriting prior
  evidence.
- Highlight added, removed, conflicting, and reclassified entities and
  relationships.
- Add reviewed SCOT session-publication views and Synapse-backed graph
  comparison where the configured authority is available.

## v0.9.4 — Candidate behavior review

**Candidate status (2026-08-31):** the immutable proposal and append-only
human-disposition authorities are implemented. Entity, relationship, and
behavior proposals require exact extracted-candidate spans; model-origin
proposals retain provider/model and prompt/response hashes without storing raw
prompts. Human acceptance requires admitted evidence, an alternative
explanation, and formal confidence with rationale, and still performs no graph
materialization or publication. The browser review workflow and optional live
model invocation remain v0.9.5 work.

- Add optional model-assisted entity, relationship, and behavior proposals.
  Every proposal must cite an extracted span and remain separate from observed
  document content until explicit analyst disposition.
- Compare observed behavior with the pinned ATT&CK, Kill Chain, and Diamond
  perspectives.
- Present unmatched behavior as a candidate behavior or framework gap, not an
  automatically discovered TTP.
- Require evidence references, alternative explanations, analyst disposition,
  and confidence rationale before materialization or publication.

## v0.9.5 — Document ingestion and usability closure

- Complete governed document ingestion for local files, bounded URLs and RSS,
  email and attachments, Office documents, images, and scanned-PDF OCR.
- Match or exceed the pinned SCOT4 Flair/entity and Vertex Synapse
  ingest/scrape baselines on the same published evaluation corpus. Report
  precision, recall, supported entity types, source-location fidelity,
  throughput, skipped inputs, and false-positive causes rather than claiming
  parity from feature counts.
- Preserve an auditable chain from original document hash through parser
  output, extracted span, normalized entity, relationship proposal, analyst
  disposition, graph admission, and SCOT/Synapse exchange receipt.
- Provide drag/drop and batch intake, extraction preview, highlighted source
  spans, duplicate/entity-resolution review, contradiction display, gap-aware
  next actions, and keyboard-accessible approve/reject controls.
- Pass hostile-document tests for active content, archive expansion, malformed
  files, path traversal, SSRF, parser exhaustion, prompt injection, embedded
  secrets, and unsupported or encrypted documents.

- Run novice, working analyst, and expert task walkthroughs at phone, laptop,
  and desktop widths.
- Measure time to start an investigation, find the next action, inspect
  provenance, resolve a contradiction, pivot, and export a report.
- Close keyboard, screen-reader, contrast, focus restoration, reduced-motion,
  terminology, and low-light issues.
- Remove or combine controls that do not improve task completion while keeping
  command and expert-workbench access.

## Cross-release minimum viable product closure

These are release obligations, not additional dashboard features. They may
land in any pre-1.0 point release, but every item needs an explicit completion
receipt or a documented preview/deferred boundary before v1.0:

- **Public identity and installation:** define one supported clean-machine
  install, update, uninstall, and version-check path. Resolve the public
  `Pivotglass` name versus the `adversary-pursuit` distribution and `ap`
  compatibility command without breaking existing users.
- **No-key golden path:** ship a deterministic, synthetic learning workspace
  that proves install, launch, intake, enrichment, provenance, contradiction
  and gap review, graph pivot, report/export, restart, and recovery without a
  paid account or contact with a third party.
- **Stable versus preview contract:** publish a compatibility and maturity
  matrix for Python, Node, browsers, workspace schemas, model providers,
  enrichment services, document formats, SCOT4, Synapse, go-roast, and
  Nucleotide. Preview integrations stay disabled by default and are not
  described as stable authority paths.
- **Data ownership and safety:** document where evidence and secrets live,
  what can leave the computer, retention and deletion behavior, backup and
  restore, sanitized support bundles, and the consequences of LAN exposure.
  No remote provider or integration receives data without an explicit,
  visible action and scope.
- **Public trust routes:** provide supported-version and vulnerability-reporting
  policy, support and issue-reporting guidance, known limitations, third-party
  license inventory, SBOM, checksums, and signed release artifacts. These
  requirements must have a manual receipt path if repository-hosted workflows
  remain intentionally absent.
- **Performance and capacity envelope:** publish and test honest supported
  limits for startup, workspace size, document size and batch size, graph and
  constellation scale, cancellation latency, memory use, and export time.
  Exceeding a limit must degrade clearly and recoverably rather than hang or
  silently omit evidence.
- **Failure and recovery rehearsal:** verify missing credentials, unavailable
  models and services, network loss, interrupted enrichment, stale web assets,
  failed migrations, corrupt or hostile documents, and integration outages.
  The local investigation remains usable and explains the next safe action.
- **Release scope decision:** either pass live, disposable round-trip and
  recovery gates for Synapse and SCOT4, or label those adapters preview for
  v1.0. Likewise, define a stable core of document formats and mark unqualified
  OCR, Office, email, RSS, or URL intake as preview rather than overstating
  coverage.

## v1.0.0 — Public release gate

- No unresolved critical or high security findings.
- Evidence, inference, proposals, narration, and presentation remain visibly
  distinct in every interface and export.
- Existing workspaces migrate forward with backup-first recovery.
- Governed document ingestion passes the shared SCOT4/Synapse comparison
  corpus with complete provenance and no unreviewed graph mutations.
- Full Python, web, package, release-contract, browser, accessibility, and
  supply-chain verification passes from a clean checkout.
- The Flint 0.4.0 qualification and rollback rehearsal pass, or the release is
  demonstrably restored to the qualified 0.3.0 baseline.
- Every cross-release MVP closure item has a completion receipt or an explicit,
  truthful preview/deferred boundary that is consistent in product, help, and
  documentation.
- Installation, first investigation, graph review, model configuration,
  integrations, reporting, and recovery documentation matches the executable
  product.
