# Pivotglass v0.8.0 through v1.0.0 roadmap

**Status:** approved; v0.8 implementation started 2026-08-01
**Baseline:** v0.7.0 (`5690f992c846`)
**Purpose:** turn a capable enrichment cockpit into a reproducible, challengeable,
and publicly trustworthy analytic system.

## Release thesis

- **v0.8.0 — Analytic Method and Evidence Integrity:** establish how Pivotglass
  knows, tests, revises, and expresses uncertainty.
- **v0.9.0 — Multiple Lenses and Connected Intelligence:** project the same
  evidence through complementary frameworks, establish the Synapse graph
  backend path, and establish SCOT4 hunt-session publication.
- **v1.0.0 — Trustworthy Public Release:** freeze, secure, document, migrate,
  and prove the supported public contract.

The dependency order is binding. Framework mappings and external graph systems
must not precede the observation, assertion, provenance, and contradiction
authorities they depend on.

## Core tenets

1. Observations are immutable; normalized entities may deduplicate.
2. Evidence, assumptions, inferences, judgments, and narration remain distinct.
3. A provider score, source quality, information credibility, likelihood,
   analytic confidence, and Dossier completeness are different measurements.
4. Contradictions are retained and investigated, not flattened into one value.
5. Models may propose; humans disposition consequential analytic work.
6. One evidence substrate supports many derived perspectives.
7. Every graph edge and framework mapping has a basis and provenance.
8. Presentation state never alters analytical truth.
9. External systems remain behind stable Pivotglass contracts. Synapse is the
   planned primary graph persistence backend after an explicit migration and
   cutover; SCOT4 is the planned hunt-results web presentation and pivot surface.
10. Existing workspaces and analyst work migrate forward without destructive
    rewriting.

## Canonical information flow

```text
sources
  -> immutable observations and provenance
  -> normalized entities and observed relationships
  -> Vertex Synapse governed graph persistence
  -> assertions, hypotheses, assumptions, support, contradiction
  -> Dossier / ATT&CK / Kill Chain / Diamond projections
  -> question-appropriate visualizations and source-backed reports
  -> reviewed SCOT4 hunt-session publication and analyst pivots
```

### W-080-02 — Immutable observation and provenance ledger

- Preserve every accepted observation even when its normalized STIX entity is
  already present.
- Record source identity, collection run, sanitized endpoint, API and collector
  versions, collection time, response digest, media type, handling marking, and
  the exact transformed object.
- Add correction, retraction, supersession, source-dependence, raw-artifact,
  and retention contracts.
- Never expose credentials or full query strings in UI payloads or exports.

### W-080-03 — Scientific investigation lifecycle

Implement one persisted lifecycle:

1. question;
2. competing hypotheses;
3. assumptions;
4. predictions and observable signposts;
5. collection plan and stop conditions;
6. observations;
7. corroborating and contradicting evidence;
8. revision, rejection, retention, or suspension;
9. conclusion, confidence, limitations, and unresolved gaps.

The existing Predictions Log and falsification engine become components of this
lifecycle and migrate without losing historical records.

### W-080-04 — Structured Analytic Technique workbench

Initial versioned protocols:

- Quality of Information Check;
- Key Assumptions Check;
- Analysis of Competing Hypotheses;
- Indicators and Signposts;
- Devil's Advocacy;
- Premortem Analysis;
- Chronology and Timeline Analysis.

Each protocol has a human-readable purpose, required inputs, required outputs,
version, state, and analyst disposition. Model-authored work remains visibly
pending until accepted, rejected, or revised by a human.

### W-080-05 — Confidence, likelihood, and contradiction authority

- Use Low, Moderate, and High for analytic confidence, with a required rationale
  grounded in logic, evidence, source quality, independence, assumptions, and
  knowledge gaps.
- Store standardized likelihood terms and probability ranges separately.
- Persist contradictions between observations, assertions, hypotheses, values,
  and time intervals.
- Record materiality, status, what evidence would resolve the contradiction,
  and the eventual resolution or supersession.
- Do not increase confidence merely because duplicate or dependent sources agree.

### W-080-06 — Analyst workbench and diagnostics

- Investigation notebook and question view.
- Hypothesis and ACH matrices.
- Assumptions register and contradiction queue.
- Prioritized intelligence requirements and next-best-information suggestions.
- Method-aware reports with evidence, alternatives, confidence, and gaps.
- Activity & Errors terminal and sanitized diagnostic details.
- Workspace authority registry and explicit offline/degraded behavior.

### W-080-07 — Evidence-grounded pursuit challenges

- Generate stable challenges for the current indicator from stored public
  reporting about infrastructure, malware, campaigns, and threat actors.
- Preserve the observation and source basis that caused each challenge to
  appear; never create analytical facts from persona narration.
- Count independent source-dependence groups rather than duplicated feeds.
- Persist progress and award one durable, self-describing badge per completed
  challenge, with simple accessible artwork.
- Show total badges and the latest award in Pivotglass; provide detailed
  `challenges` and `badges` commands across interfaces.

### v0.8.0 release gate

- Assumptions, alternatives, contradictions, and knowledge gaps remain visible.

- Every method run is named and versioned.

- Existing workspaces migrate with verified backup and recovery.
- An exported investigation can reproduce its analytic record without the

### W-090-01 — Framework projection authority

Every mapping records framework/content version, evidence basis, mapper and
version, automated-vs-human origin, confidence rationale, analyst override, and
revocation or supersession state. Mappings are derived views, never source truth.

### W-090-02 — ATT&CK, Kill Chain, and Diamond perspectives

- Version-pinned MITRE ATT&CK content, evidence-backed mappings, gap views, and
  Navigator export.
- Cyber Kill Chain phase perspective with explicit ambiguity and looping.
- Diamond events containing adversary, capability, infrastructure, victim,
  meta-features, confidence, unknown vertices, activity threads, and groups.
- Framework gaps become intelligence requirements, never fabricated evidence.

Implementation receipt: ATT&CK content is pinned to the official v19.2 STIX
bundle by URL and SHA-256 and is never downloaded silently. TUI and Pivotglass
share `framework manifest|list|show|map|accept|reject|revoke|navigator`.
Continuously polled web state exposes only versions and disposition counts;
mapping evidence and rationale require an explicit command. `framework
require` turns an explicit unsupported framework item into a scored collection
requirement, refuses accepted mappings, and remains idempotent.

### W-090-03 — Two-layer graph workspace

1. **Entity graph:** indicators, infrastructure, actors, capabilities, victims,
   events, and observed relationships.
2. **Epistemic graph:** observations, assertions, hypotheses, assumptions,
   mappings, and supports/contradicts/depends-on/supersedes relationships.

Add expand/collapse, pin, multiselect, annotations, manual analyst assertions,
undo/redo, filters, saved presentation layouts, and STIX/GEXF/JSON/CSV export.

Implementation receipt: selecting exactly two entity nodes can create a
directional analyst relation only through the shared analytic ledger. Both
endpoints must exist, the relationship vocabulary is bounded, and an annotation
is mandatory. The resulting active human judgment is drawn as a manual edge
and counted by the matching degree view, but is never persisted as an observed
STIX relationship.

Active manual edges can also be revised or retracted through the same command
authority. Revision creates a replacement assertion with an explicit
supersession link; retraction withdraws the active edge. Both require a human
reason, append lifecycle correction history, preserve the original assertion,
and leave observations untouched.

The shared layered exporter produces exact JSON, spreadsheet-safe CSV, and
Gephi-ready GEXF for all layers or an explicit entity, epistemic, or bridge
scope. It preserves edge truth kind, provenance, rationale, and direction.
Bridge-only exports include both endpoint node layers. Existing STIX export
remains the entity/evidence exchange rather than misrepresenting analytic
notebook records as observations.

### W-090-04 — Visualization advisor

Select from the analyst question, data shape, scale, uncertainty, missingness,
cardinality, viewport, interaction, and accessibility requirements. Complete:

- adjustable histograms;
- scatter/PCA with explained variance and exposed points;
- dendrogram and sunburst hierarchy views;
- contradiction and ACH matrices;
- uncertainty displays and small multiples;
- exact-data tables and exports;
- a visible explanation of why each visualization was selected.

A force-directed graph shows relationships. PCA shows similarity among numeric
features. Neither is a substitute for the other.

Implementation receipt: the indicator-coverage PCA view excludes deferred,
unavailable, and zero-variance dimensions without imputation, requires at
least three indicators and two comparable varying dimensions, reports both
axes' explained variance, and exports the exact standardized source profile
with every point. It does not create or imply a graph edge.

Implementation receipt: the competing-hypotheses matrix requires at least two
hypotheses and one directly linked observation or assertion. It projects only
persisted analyst stances, preserves mixed supporting/contradicting links, and
marks missing stances as not assessed rather than neutral.

Implementation receipt: the native investigation hierarchy is derived only
from persisted scientific-lifecycle membership. It presents workspace,
investigation, question, hypothesis, and other workflow paths in accessible
collapsible branches, includes path and depth in exact-data export, and does
not imply support, causality, attribution, or confidence.

Implementation receipt: the native uncertainty view renders only validated
persisted likelihood intervals, carries likelihood rationale and assessor, and
shows the latest separate confidence level, rationale, and assessor without a
numeric conversion or combined score. Invalid intervals are omitted and
counted rather than guessed. Repeated assessment rows use the same fixed axis
and redundant text, providing the required accessible small-multiple view.

### W-090-07 — go-roast reverse-OAST analysis

**Implementation status (0.9 work package):** A shell-free local adapter uses
go-roast's JSON boundary for bounded decode and campaign analysis. Successful
operations emit secret-safe receipts. Decoded domains become review-only node
and typed-relationship proposals for campaign, machine, and process fragments;
every result states that these fragments, counter patterns, and timezone
estimates are correlations rather than identity proof. Analysts can record
idempotent proposals into the scientific lifecycle and must explicitly accept
or reject each with a rationale; review history and caveats remain attached.
A second explicit action materializes an accepted relationship proposal as an
inferred assertion, retains the proposal and receipt, and does not create an
observation or observed graph edge. Exact domain matches now cite immutable
workspace observations and appear as structural provenance links in the
epistemic graph. A separate correlation review groups shared fragments by
persisted proposal and flags incompatible decoder targets; source diversity is
reported without converting it into identity or formal confidence.

- Preserve decoded timestamp, machine fragment, PID fragment, counter,

### W-090-08 — Nucleotide traffic analysis

**Implementation status (0.9 work package):** A shell-free local adapter uses a
configured, versioned lookup JSON for URL attribution and Nucleotide's
JSONL-to-YAML actor-fingerprint boundary for analyst-grouped events. It retains
ambiguous matches, lookup SHA-256, signals, contradictions, inferred options,
template preferences, and caveats. Detection content remains review-only and
is never deployed by Pivotglass. Lookup outcomes and fingerprint summaries can
be recorded as pending, idempotent external-analysis proposals and
dispositioned only by an explicit human action.
Accepted results require a second explicit action before they become inferred
assertions. The lookup/fingerprint digest, proposal, caveats, and analyst
rationale remain linked, and no source observation is fabricated.
Exact matching URL observations are cited by recorded lookup proposals and
appear as structural provenance links; a match remains derived attribution and
not proof that Nuclei generated the request.
Persisted fingerprints can be listed by analyst-grouped batch and compared
through Nucleotide's published field-by-field diff. Pivotglass discloses when
the lookup corpora differ and creates neither formal confidence nor identity.

- Treat URL-to-template matches and tool/CLI choices as derived hypotheses,
  not observations or actor attribution.
- Preserve observability tier, lookup source/commit, ambiguity, structural

## v1.0.0 — Trustworthy Public Release

### Stable public contracts

Freeze and document workspace migrations, evidence and analytic records, module
adapters, visualization intents, framework projections, external exchange
envelopes, model proposals, analyst disposition, and export formats.

### Reliability and security

- Migration tests from every supported release.
- Backup, recovery, corruption detection, conflict handling, and cancellation.
- Secret redaction and credential isolation.
- Prompt-injection separation between collected content and instructions.
- Path traversal, hostile payload, HTML injection, CSV formula injection, and
  malformed STIX tests.
- Threat models for local web exposure, models, plugins, Synapse, SCOT, logs,
  imports, and exports.
- SBOM, signed artifacts, checksums, dependency review, and reproducible builds.

### Usability and accessibility

- TUI and Pivotglass behavioral parity.
- Keyboard-only and screen-reader workflows.
- 320 px, laptop, and desktop verification.
- No clipped overlays, focus traps, or invisible keyboard owners.
- Novice, Adept, and Expert help levels.
- Activity & Errors terminal, reduced motion, high contrast, and accessible chart
  tables.
- Junior and expert walkthroughs using identical seeded investigations.

### Documentation and trust

- Quick start, analyst methodology, administrator, migration, integration, data
  handling, trust model, and troubleshooting guides.
- Worked cases that show evidence, assumptions, contradictions, SAT execution,
  alternatives, framework projections, confidence, and reporting.
- Explicit limitations: Pivotglass assists analysis; it does not autonomously
  establish attribution or verdicts.
- SemVer, deprecation, schema-support, and vulnerability-reporting policies.

## Explicitly outside the v1.0 critical path

- Additional character modes and arcade games.
- A 3D Dossier decahedron unless usability testing proves analytic value.
- Public competition, career ranking, federation, and cloud collaboration.
- Ungoverned RAG over arbitrary material.
- Unreviewed Synapse graph mutation or unapproved SCOT publication.
- Autonomous attribution or arbitrary model-generated graph edges.
- Rotatable 3D PCA as the default relationship view.

Reusable playbooks, a governed knowledge library, MCP access, and federation may
be designed during v0.9, but they do not block the trustworthy local v1.0 contract.
