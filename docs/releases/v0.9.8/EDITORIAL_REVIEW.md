# v0.9.8 documentation review

Reviewed 2026-09-27 by an AI editorial crew acting in experienced security-analysis
and newcomer/editor roles, with repository-owner direction. These are role-based
AI reviews, not independent human endorsements or a measured usability study.

## Coverage and method

All public root and `docs/` Markdown was inventoried and reviewed for navigation,
branding, story, audience, authority boundaries, and current-versus-historical
claims. Current operator contracts were compared with commands, ledger/SAT
protocols, source-admission logic, learning fixtures, and browser behavior.
Historical QA, release, announcement, and design records received editorial
review; long historical plans used whole-file headings/keyword/paragraph scans
and substantive section inspection. Their old test counts, commit hashes, and
publication receipts were preserved rather than independently recertified.
Protected private design context and the independent career project were excluded.

## Changes and findings

- Added a practical product story and task-based routes through the guide.
- Added current architecture, scientific-process and SAT walkthroughs, a synthetic
  worked case, visualization interpretation, and a gradual learning exercise.
- Corrected outdated graph navigation, saved-state scope, indicator truncation,
  candidate pagination, optional cloud speech, and database-only recovery claims.
- Distinguished planned remote document/parser controls and backend cutover from
  current capabilities. SAT validation checks required structure; analysts perform
  technique interpretation and dispositions. Layout proximity does not prove control.
- Explained that guidance-level selection is manual and practice is not proof of
  expertise. Claimed benefits describe mechanisms, not measured productivity gains.
- Moved dated release records, large planning history, and generated decisions into
  purpose-named directories; repaired relative links and added a navigation checker.
- Retained the v0.9.5 video as explicitly historical. New graph and Q&A screenshots
  show a synthetic v0.9.8 case; they do not claim a refreshed full video walkthrough.
- Retired executable and distribution names in historical prose use labeled
  placeholders. A narrow serialized-extension reader and explicit prior-home path
  retain the identifiers needed to recover existing evidence.

The inventory checker scans all public Markdown local paths and heading anchors.
It does not validate remote websites, prose truth, or external live integrations.
