I built Pivotglass to help cyber defenders level up. Analysts have to work
through incomplete data, make careful decisions, and leave behind breadcrumbs
that another analyst can follow. Too often, that work gets scattered across
tools, tabs, and notes.

Pivotglass is my attempt to make the job faster and easier to accomplish while
keeping it rigorous. It's an AI-augmented framework with repeatable workflows:
start with a question, review a source, choose IoCs to ingest, pivot through the
relationships, test competing hypotheses, and produce a report with the
evidence and uncertainty still visible.

Built-in provenance history shows how you got to the current state. Relationship graphs distinguish
stored connections, annotations, and indicators grouped by an analyst. Structured
Analytic Techniques help expose assumptions and contradictions. A Novice Q&A workflow
helps newer analysts practice asking better investigative questions. None of
those features turns an LLM's answers into evidence or takes the final judgment
away from the analyst.

My goal is simple: Use AI and automation to help defenders excel and work more
consistently, learn while doing it, and produce results they can verify and
hand off. With recognizable themes and generative music scores, hopefully they will even enjoy the work a bit more.

I made a short Overview video showing the interface and the workflow. If this
sounds useful, try Pivotglass and tell me what works and what needs work!
