# v0.6 arcade synthetic playtest

**Method:** adversarial design review using five simulated teen-gamer
perspectives. These are product-review lenses, not claims of real human
participants or usability research.

| Review lens | What failed in the prior build | Release response | Still worth testing with people |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Speedrunner | Fixed maps and no run metrics made mastery meaningless. | Jack In now scores efficient routing, exposes trace and objectives, supports same-map retry, and advances through eight bounded levels. | Route exploits, optimal-score balance, and whether restarts feel immediate enough. |
| Puzzle gamer | Most answers, shutdown orders, chess positions, and layouts repeated after one attempt. | Triage has ten shuffled cases, Troll has nine shuffled claims, HAL has four incidents, Sherlock has two positions, Matrix grids are seeded, and Jack In generates seeded solvable layouts. | Expand the chess library and verify every future puzzle with a chess engine. |
| Roguelite player | There was no escalating run, resource pressure, risk/reward decision, or reason to start again. | Jack In adds data-cache objectives, a trace budget, ICE penalties, efficiency bonuses, level progression, retry, and a new-identity run. | Persistent unlocks should remain cosmetic and must never imply analytical achievement. |
| Arcade/action player | The 4×4 corner walk lacked decisions and spectacle. | Boards grow from 5×5 to 7×7, layouts vary, ICE density and cache count rise, feedback is immediate, and the HUD makes the run legible. | Optional patrol ICE and combo systems, provided reduced-motion and turn-based alternatives remain equal. |
| Accessibility-conscious player | A twitch-heavy redesign could exclude keyboard, touch, or reduced-motion users and steal analyst input. | Jack In is turn-based, supports arrows/buttons/Enter/R, labels every grid cell, reports status through an ARIA live region, and installs no global key handler. Game state stays inside the modal. | Screen-reader walkthrough, switch-control testing, and touch target review on physical devices. |

## Verification contract

- All random layout and content helpers are pure and seeded.
- One hundred seeds at every Jack In level are checked for reachable exits and
  reachable data objectives.
- Level, board, cache, obstacle, and trace bounds are tested.
- Presentation randomness never enters evidence, dossier, command,
  investigation, or confidence state.
- Closing the arcade discards its local run; it cannot change the cockpit.

## Deferred backlog

- Real moderated playtests across novice and experienced players.
- A verified chess-puzzle corpus substantially larger than the two-position
  release pool.
- More HAL incidents and richer Matrix topology.
- Optional Neuromancer enemy patrols, route previews, and combo scoring only
  after accessibility and focus regression tests exist.
