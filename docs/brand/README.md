# Pivotglass — Digital Looking Glass

![Pivotglass wordmark](pivotglass-looking-glass-v1.png)

The angled glass portal invites a change of perspective. A mirrored digital city lives inside it; nodes and shards suggest evidence paths and new questions. Cyan and violet retain the cyberdeck atmosphere. The wordmark should remain legible; use the emblem when space is limited.

- [Horizontal wordmark](pivotglass-looking-glass-v1.png)
- [Transparent portal emblem](pivotglass-looking-glass-emblem-v1.png)

Original AI-generated artwork created on 2026-09-29 from the requested “Digital Alice Through the Looking Glass” concept. The brief asked for an original digital mirror/portal, geometric shards and connected evidence nodes, cyan/violet light on navy, and a readable PIVOTGLASS wordmark; no film character or existing brand was supplied. The companion brief requested the same portal as a standalone transparent emblem without lettering. The emblem is an RGBA PNG. These are raster brand candidates, not vector master artwork or a trademark clearance claim.

Use the wordmark on dark presentation surfaces and the emblem for thumbnails. Preserve proportions and keep other text outside the mark. The artwork is presentation and does not carry analytical meaning.
